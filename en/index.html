What I get asked most

Why a "from" price?

Because the work changes with the number of applications, whether a staging environment exists and the state of the documentation. The exact amount is on the quote, and it does not move once signed.

What is never included?

Application development, on-call, 24/7 monitoring and any availability guarantee: I do not monitor your service.

What happens if there is an incident during the work?

Nothing is changed before a backup has been restored and the rollback proven. Should an incident happen anyway, I apply the rollback prepared for the step in progress and your service returns to its previous state.
