About

Arthur Bernard DevOps & Cloud engineer

Hi, I'm Arthur. I'm an independent DevOps and Cloud engineer, based just outside Paris. I take care of getting your application into production and keeping it running. At Alan, the health insurer, I led the migration of a production serving over a million members, with no downtime. I also run the infrastructure of Glasck.gg, an esports platform I co-founded, and I operate Suiton.app, my own public application.

Before IT, I was a pastry chef: Lenôtre, the Plaza Athénée, La Maison du Chocolat, then a year running a kitchen in Australia. Ten years leading teams under service constraints that were never negotiable. I retrained and went back to study at École 42 in 2024, in systems and networks. My engagements run from one day to six weeks, on production systems that are already running. If that is your situation, write to me.
