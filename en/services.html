Case studies

Three production environments

Alan, frontend production migration

Cloudflare · Workers · Terraform

The platform hosting the frontend production was reaching end of life. At a health insurer, no downtime window is negotiable. I drove the switch to Workers application by application, writing the cutover and rollback scripts myself, proving the rollback before every step, then reconciling the routes into infrastructure as code before decommissioning the old platform. Critical surfaces, including alan.com, went through with no interruption observed.

Glasck, esport data platform

Docker · Traefik · PostgreSQL · Prometheus

A team of four, real traffic, and one single person able to deploy. I designed and still maintain the whole infrastructure: Docker Compose, Traefik with automated TLS, PostgreSQL with connection pooling, Redis, network segmentation and Prometheus and Grafana monitoring, all deployable with one command. Anyone on the team can now bring the service back up, which was the point: not replacing one single point of failure with another.

Suiton, a public product in operation

CI/CD · Supabase · PWA

A product open to the public that I have operated on my own since launch: domain and TLS, automated deployment on every push, installable app, 18 languages, per-user access rules, backups and scheduled jobs. It is my permanent operations ground, and where I handle real incidents, such as a cache serving stale code after a deployment that was losing users their settings. The application code is generated with AI; the production setup and the operations are mine.

FAQ

What people ask before signing

Why a "from" price rather than a firm one?
  • The number of applications and services involved
  • Whether a staging environment exists
  • Access available and a named contact
  • The state of the existing documentation
  • The acceptable downtime window and how urgent it is
What is never included?
  • Application development
  • On-call and 24/7 monitoring
  • Any service level commitment on your production
  • Design, content and search optimisation
Is the thirty-day warranty free maintenance?

No. The warranty covers non-conformities on the delivered scope, meaning anything that does not do what the quote said it would. It does not cover changes, incidents caused by edits made after acceptance, or day-to-day operations. Thirty days rather than three months because an infrastructure non-conformity shows up in the first days of operation. Ongoing support is a separate contract.

What happens if there is an incident during the work?

Nothing is changed before a backup has been restored and the rollback proven. Every step can be undone on its own, and the intervention window is agreed in writing beforehand.

What are the payment terms?

30 % deposit on order, balance on acceptance, after written approval of the delivery.

What happens to your access to our systems afterwards?

Access is named, created by you, and revoked at handover. The revocation is confirmed in writing. I never hold an owner account or a shared credential.
